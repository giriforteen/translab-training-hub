# Module 7: Handling Difficult Situations

**Session 7 of 24 • Pillar #7 • Strength Layer**

## Overview

This module teaches PMs how to handle the four types of difficult situations they face daily:
- 💰 **Money Conflicts** - Payment issues, budget cuts
- 📊 **Scope Battles** - Unrealistic additions, creep
- 👥 **People Problems** - Team departures, conflicts
- 🎭 **Political Conflicts** - Stakeholder wars

## The Framework: CALM → CLARIFY → CONTROL

**CALM (2 minutes)**
- Pause and breathe
- Assess true urgency
- Take 5 minutes to think

**CLARIFY (5 minutes)**
- What exactly happened?
- What's the real impact?
- What do I control?

**CONTROL (Action)**
- Fix it yourself (within authority)
- Escalate with context
- Negotiate new terms
- Accept and adapt

## Structure (9 Sections)

1. **Welcome to Strength** - Foundation complete, building muscle
2. **The Framework** - CALM → CLARIFY → CONTROL breakdown
3. **Knowledge Check** - 3 questions on framework
4. **Scenario 1: Money Conflict** - "Can't pay milestone, finish anyway"
5. **Scenario 2: Scope Battle** - "Add 15 features, same deadline"
6. **Scenario 3: People Problem** - "I quit. Last day Friday."
7. **Scenario 4: Political Conflict** - Two stakeholders, conflicting orders
8. **Templates & Tools** - Summary of all templates
9. **Commitment & Completion** - Weekly commitment + download
