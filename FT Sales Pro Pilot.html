Episode 0 · The Real World
What is IT Sales
really like?
Let us tell you a true story from our own team.

One morning, a client called. They needed 300 laptops. With OS installed. By Friday. It was Wednesday.

The sales team said yes. Then the real work began.

1
📞
The Call
A big client in Bengaluru calls. 300 laptops needed. By Friday. The sales rep takes a deep breath and says — "Yes. We will do it."
2
🚨
The Problem
300 laptops. Not in the Bengaluru warehouse. Chennai procurement team gets the call. Orders placed. Delivery tracked. The clock is running.
3
🚚
The Arrival
Thursday morning. The laptops arrive. All 300 of them. Boxes everywhere. The Bengaluru office now looks like a warehouse. And then the real work starts.
4
💻
The Night Shift
The office becomes a testing station. OS installation in progress — on all 300. Night shifts. Chai. Music. The team works like a startup launching their first product.
5
☕
The Grind
2am. 100 done. 200 to go. Someone orders biryani. Someone plays music. Nobody goes home. This is not just a job. This is the FT spirit.
6
🎉
Friday Morning
All 300 laptops. Delivered. Installed. Tested. On time. The client is delighted. The invoice is raised. And that client? They called again three weeks later.
This is IT sales. Messy. Real. Human.
And when you get it right — deeply satisfying.
